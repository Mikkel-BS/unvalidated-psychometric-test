How the scoring works

Each trait has six statements. Responses are scored 1–5; reverse-keyed statements are flipped. The mean is then rescaled to 0–100. A score of 50 therefore means the midpoint of the response scale, not “average compared with people.” Interpretive ranges below 40, 40–60, and above 60 are editorial conveniences, not empirically established cutoffs. Six answers can hide a mix of responses, so treat each paragraph as a prompt for reflection rather than a finding.

A one-step change to one answer moves its trait score by about four points. Small differences should not be treated as meaningful distinctions. The twelve themes may overlap; they have not been shown to be independent factors.

Every question and scale description in this project is original. The model borrows only broad, non-proprietary ideas from trait psychology. Questionnaire version 0.2.0.
